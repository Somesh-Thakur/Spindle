Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Spindle is a free music player at spindle-bice.vercel.app. This page explains what we store about you, why, and how to remove it. In short: we keep only what's needed to sign you in and sync your library between your devices. We don't show ads and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and the name you choose. If you sign up with email, your password is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider; we never see it.
  • Google or GitHub sign-in: if you use one, we receive your name, email address and profile picture link from that service. We don't get access to your Google or GitHub data beyond that, and we don't post anything on your behalf.
  • Your library: liked songs, playlists (including any cover image you upload), lyrics you add, your settings (such as region and display options) and the queue you're listening to, so they can follow you to your other devices.

We don't collect your location, contacts, payment details or listening history beyond the recently played list shown to you in the app.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To sync your library across the devices where you're signed in.
  • To email you a verification code when you create an account or reset your password. We don't send marketing emails.

Where it's stored

Accounts and libraries are stored with Supabase (servers in Singapore), and the website is hosted by Vercel. Each library can only be read by the account that owns it. Spindle also saves some data in your browser's local storage, such as your library and cached search results, so pages load quickly.

Services that play the music

Spindle doesn't host any music. Songs, artwork and lyrics are loaded from YouTube (through its embedded player), Apple's iTunes search, Audius, LRCLIB and lyrics.ovh. When you play or search for something, your browser contacts these services directly, and their own privacy policies apply, including Google's privacy policy for YouTube. Fonts come from Google Fonts.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account in Spindle. This permanently removes your account and your synced library. "Sign out and clear" removes your library from the device you're using. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.

Children

Spindle isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
