Good to know

Is Spindle really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription and no limits.

Where does the music come from?

Full songs play through YouTube's official player, with Apple's 30-second previews and independent artists on Audius as fallbacks. Lyrics come from LRCLIB. Spindle doesn't host or sell any music.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open Spindle in Safari or Chrome and choose "Add to Home Screen" to use it like an app.

How do I bring my Spotify playlists?

Sign in, open Import, and paste a playlist link. For private playlists and Liked Songs, export them with a free tool like Exportify and drop the file in.
